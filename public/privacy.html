Horizon Search

Privacy Notice

Updated: October 28, 2026

Draft for review. The hosting provider, its access-log practices, retention periods, and launch-specific legal requirements must be confirmed before public launch.

This notice describes the current Horizon Search application. It is operated by Teja Thuppeda. For privacy questions or requests, email tejatuppada82@gmail.com.

1. Information involved in a search

When you submit a search, the query, selected Brave-backed category, and results page number are sent to the Horizon Search server. The server sends the query and relevant search options to Brave Search to retrieve results. Brave processes that information under its own terms and privacy practices.

Maps and Shopping are links to Google. Your browser sends the query to Google only if you choose one of those links. Search-result links also take you to third-party sites, which then receive information according to their own services and policies.

2. Browser and network details

Search terms appear in the results-page URL and may remain in your browser history. Avoid using sensitive information in a query if you do not want it recorded by your browser or exposed when sharing that URL. When image thumbnails are shown, your browser may request them from Brave’s image-hosting service.

The current application does not create user accounts, use analytics, or intentionally store search history. It temporarily keeps the connecting network address in server memory for per-client rate limiting; expired entries are pruned as later requests are handled. The application does not use cookies for this functionality.

3. Storage and service providers

The application has no search-history database or search-result cache. The public hosting provider has not yet been selected or confirmed. Hosting, network, and security providers may process connection data or maintain access logs; their identities, purposes, and retention periods must be added after the deployment is chosen. Do not treat this draft as a promise about provider-side retention.

4. Choices and requests

You can avoid submitting a query, avoid opening third-party links, and clear your browser history using your browser’s controls. Questions or privacy requests may be sent to tejatuppada82@gmail.com. Requests will be handled as required by applicable law.

5. Changes

This notice may be updated when the service or its providers change. The updated date above will be revised when a new version is published.

This is a working draft, not legal advice. Confirm hosting and provider data practices and obtain qualified legal review before public launch. See the Terms & Conditions.
